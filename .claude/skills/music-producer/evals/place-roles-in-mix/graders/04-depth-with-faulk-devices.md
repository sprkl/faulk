Depth is given per part as a plane (front, middle or back, or the French equivalents) and built with Faulk's Reverb (size, damping, predelay, mix), Echo (division or time, feedback, mix) and Filter (mode, cutoff) on the part's own track, with values inside the devices' ranges (size, damping and mix from 0 to 1, Echo feedback at most 0.95, Reverb predelay at most 0.2 s); the kick and the bass are not on the back plane, and the back plane is darker (a lower Filter cutoff or a higher Reverb damping) than the front.
