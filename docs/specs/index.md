# Specs

* [Atelier et modules](01-atelier-modules.md) - Proposal for the Atelier: anatomy of an audio plugin mapped to a minimal Faulk module ABI (a 20-byte event whose note-on carries its fine tuning, #219) and manifest, synthesis and effect families, a three-level editor (block graph, Faust, Rust) with escape hatches, an AI create-listen-measure loop, sandboxing in both runtimes, and a concrete MVP.
* [Découpage du MVP](02-decoupage-mvp.md) - Split of the Faulk MVP (MIDI and audio tracks, automation, several instruments and effects, in both modes) into 96 tasks for a swarm of agents, with the founder's decisions of 2026-10-14: socle decisions, epics and tasks with their tags, phases, dependency graph, critical path, contracts to freeze before parallel work, and conflict hot spots.
* [Enregistrement MIDI](03-enregistrement-midi.md) - Spec of MIDI recording into a clip: arming, count-in, metronome, new clip versus existing clip, overdub versus replace, quantise on input, undo granularity, latency compensation in Web and Boost, the commands it needs and what agents can do with them; open product questions marked.
