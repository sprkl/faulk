* **Update** (#703): `clip.audio.set_range` and `transport.set_tempo` no longer leave an audio placement ending past its clip or overlapping another one. A range change refuses (naming the placement) when a placement that plays part of the clip would end past the new length or when exact lengths (an undo's) would overlap; a tempo that would make two audio placements of a track overlap is refused, naming both. `clip.restore_audio` checks `offset + length` against the clip's frames. A deterministic property test (import, set_range, set_tempo, place, delete, undo, redo) checks that every delete is undone. Looped audio clips keep the strict rule until AU-6 (#115).
