* **Update** (#407): the master's automation lanes are now editable in the arrangement timeline. A "Master" row is pinned at the bottom of the track list in `ArrangementView.svelte` (not deletable, not movable). It unfolds like a track row to show its lanes; lanes can be added from the arrangement's add-lane selector, drawn (click to add a point), moved and deleted. The "Automate" button appears on the master fader in `MixerStrip.svelte` and on each effect knob in `DeviceCard.svelte` (when `master={true}`): it creates the lane if absent, then navigates to the arrangement and unfolds the master. The unfold is a one-shot request on the ui store (`ui.showMasterLanes()` sets `openMasterLanes`, `ArrangementView` consumes it with `takeMasterLanesRequest()`), so a user who folds the master and navigates back is not overridden. All operations use the commands of #378 (`automation.lane.create` with `master: true`, `automation.lane.delete`, `automation.points.*`). New i18n keys in `automation.ts`: `showMaster`, `hideMaster`, `addLaneForMaster`, `automate`, `automateHint` (EN and FR). The e2e test `automation: master row in the timeline, a fade drawn, level confirmed` draws the volume fade on the master by pointer (two points, a step curve, a point moved and deleted then undone), checks lane delete and undo, exercises the mixer and device-knob "Automate" buttons (idempotent), and confirms that `data-master-peak` falls below 1 % of its loud value after one bar at 120 BPM.
