* **Update** (#402): the engine's output goes through an always-on safety limiter (`engine::limiter`, last stage of `Engine::render`): ceiling -0.3 dBFS, stereo-linked, immediate attack on the sample that goes over (no look-ahead, so 0 frames of added latency: proven at the stage level — the over is caught on its own sample and nothing is shifted — and backed at the engine level by two independent rigs whose onset frame matches, which shows the render topology buffers nothing around the limiter), exponential release of 80 ms that snaps to unity (a signal under the ceiling passes bit for bit). It sits after the output's meter: the master meter keeps showing the level before the protection, so an overload stays visible; the speakers, the helper's callback and the WAV export (same `Engine::render`) get the protected signal, in every format (the floats included, so an export never clips and its clipped counter stays at 0). No command, no message, no protocol change. A non-finite sample leaves as silence. The gain is not reset on a project swap (retiring tracks keep their gain, avoiding a click); it resets after an all-sound-off, and follows `Engine::set_sample_rate`. The keep coefficient is capped below 1.0 to guarantee the release makes progress at any sample rate accepted by `SampleRate`. Cost measured at 48 kHz, 128-frame block: 197 ns at rest, 755 ns while limiting (0.007 % and 0.028 % of the block budget).
