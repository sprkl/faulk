* **Update** (#378): the master has automation lanes. `Project::master_automation` holds them (files `master/automation/<lane>.tsv`, same form as a track's lane; format version unchanged, still 1: a project without master lanes writes the same files as before and an older folder reads with none). Targets: the master's gain and pan, and the parameters of the effects of its chain; no mute lane (a track has none). `automation.lane.create` and `automation.lane.restore` take `track` or `master: true`, `automation.lane.delete` answers `track: null` for a master lane, the lane events carry `track: null`, and `device.remove` / `device.restore` take the lanes of a master effect with them. The engine addresses the master's lanes with the track slot `u32::MAX` in `AutomationLaneUpload` and plays them with the code of a track's lanes (sub-block by sub-block, the fader's law, stop and seek, manual gestures). `PROTOCOL_VERSION` is 10. The page marks the master fader and the master effects' knobs that a lane drives (#388). Editing master lanes in the timeline is not done yet.
