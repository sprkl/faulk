* **Update** (#709): `transport.set_tempo` no longer refuses a tempo that would make two audio placements overlap (#703's refusal blocked every speed-up on back-to-back audio clips). Starts stay on their tick; an audio placement that would run past the next audio placement of its track is shortened, in whole frames, to end where that one starts (the last one put down wins, as in the arrangement gestures). Undo restores the exact lengths through an internal `placements` field of the inverse command (not in the schema). Only the absolute bound (an audio placement ending past 2^40 ticks) still refuses. The property tests (`audio_placements`, `arrangement_audio`) now start from contiguous placements and check the invariants after every tempo step.
