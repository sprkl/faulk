* **Fix** (#424): the two e2e specs reported flaky under load, `timegrid` (Ctrl + wheel) and `storage-commands` (French), failed on a wait that is paced by the machine and not by a reaction of the page. Reproduced with `--repeat-each --fully-parallel` and busy threads alongside (24 logical processors): `timegrid` waited 5 s (Playwright's default) for the dev server's module graph to load, 7 runs of 24 failed at `open()` with 8 workers and 60 busy threads; `storage-commands` waited 5 s for the engine to start (`start()`), 39 runs of 64 failed with 16 workers and 160 busy threads. The earlier hardening (hover readout, user activation) was not the cause: the `runWithoutClick` clock of 300 ms passed 48 runs of 48 under the same load. `web/tests/e2e/support.ts` now has `BOOT`, a 30 s ceiling for what the page does once on its way up; the waits for the boot of the two specs use it, and `timegrid.spec.ts` gets a test timeout of 90 s (the default 30 s would cut the ceiling short). Same load after the change: 0 failures in 24 (`timegrid`), 7 in 64 (`storage-commands`, all at a starved machine: 3 engine starts past 30 s, 4 waits past the test's 90 s). The other specs that wait for the engine with the default 5 s have the same exposure (not changed here).
