2026-10-31
* **Update** (#412): the master has a pan. New command `mixer.set_master_pan` (`edit`, undoable by inverse, pan from -1 to 1, refused outside, same contract as `mixer.set_pan`); engine sync needed no change (`MixerChanged` with no track already sends `SetMaster`, which carries the pan). The master strip shows a pan control with its readout (typed value, drag as one history group, double click to centre) driven by the master's pan lane when there is one. The "Automate" buttons of #407 are now on every strip and device knob, not only the master's: the track strip has one under its pan and one under its fader, and the track's device cards have one per knob; each creates the lane if absent (never a second one), then opens the arrangement with that owner's lanes unfolded. The one-shot request of #407 became `ui.showLanes(owner)` / `ui.takeLanesRequest()` (an owner that no longer exists is ignored, the request is always cleared). `crates/test-support/tests/master_pan.rs` renders the master pan hard left, hard right, centre and undone, natively and in WASM; `web/tests/e2e/master-pan.spec.ts` exercises the gesture.
