* **Update** (#882): step entry in the piano roll. A toolbar button and Alt+E switch a mode in which the musical-typing letters write a note at the free cursor (of the toolbar's length, through `clip.notes.add`, one undo per note) instead of playing live, and the cursor advances when the last letter held is released (letters held together are a chord). Period is a rest, Comma lengthens the last step by a drawing step, Backspace removes the last step; the three are listed in the shortcut list. `piano-roll-step.spec.ts` writes a phrase, a chord, a rest, a lengthened note and a Backspace, then undoes note by note.
