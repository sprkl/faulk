* **Update** (#916): step entry (#882, #899) stays armed from track to track and across the panel tabs: with no clip open (a track without a clip, an audio track, the Clips, Devices or Keyboard tab) the letters play notes, and a clip that opens makes them write again; `UiStore.stepWriting` (set by the roll when it has a clip) tells the keyboard player which. Only Alt+E, its button, Escape (also from inside the roll, where it still clears the selection) or musical typing switched off end it. The empty roll and a badge in the bottom panel's tab bar say "Step entry on: letters play" (or "audio off" when the audio is not started), and each switch between playing and writing is said. #899 ended the mode with the clip because the letters would then have written nothing and played nothing.
