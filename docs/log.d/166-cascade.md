* **Update** (#166): the registry gains the MIDI effect Cascade (`faulk.cascade`, kind 17, protocol 62: an arpeggiator that walks the held notes on the song's grid, from tick 0, one step per `rate` (1/1 to 1/64, dotted or triplet), in seven orders (up, down, up-down, down-up, as played, a seeded random, chord), over 1 to 4 octaves with 1 to 4 repeats, a gate of 1 to 200 % of the step, the played or a fixed velocity with an optional accent, a latch, and a pattern that restarts at each new chord or follows the grid). With the transport stopped the device keeps its own clock; a stop ends the notes sounding and the latched notes. Every note it starts is in a table with its end, so no note is left on by a setting changed mid-note, a removal or a bypass. Three factory presets.
