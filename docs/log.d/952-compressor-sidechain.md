* **Update** (#952, protocol 60 provisional, folder format 8): a Compressor on a track can listen to another track for its detection (`device.set_sidechain`, the device's `sidechain` field, the descriptor's `sidechain` flag): the source is heard after its devices and before its volume, pan and mute, rendered first by the engine (`engine::sidechain`), and deleting it sets its listeners back to their own track (undone by `track.restore`'s `listeners`). `docs/architecture/02-modele-projet.md` describes the field and format 8; the migration from format 7 changes only the number.
