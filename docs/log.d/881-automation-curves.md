* **Update** (#881, protocol 58, folder format 7): an automation point's shape gains `ease_in` (slow then fast), `ease_out` (fast then slow) and `smooth` (S-curve), the same line as `linear` warped in time (`t²`, `t (2 − t)`, `t² (3 − 2t)`), played by the engine (`engine::automation::warp`, `msg::Curve` 2 to 4) and drawn by the lane as it is played. A lane file writes the shape's word (`linear` and `step` unchanged), a lane blob its byte (0 to 4); the migration from format 6 changes only the number. `docs/architecture/02-modele-projet.md` describes the lane file's shape column; the music-producer skill's `references/notation.md` names the Faulk curve of each sheet curve.
* **Update** (#881, founder's decision): a lane on a device parameter of logarithmic scale (a frequency, a time) goes along its knob, in the logarithm of the value, for every shape (`engine::automation::Knob`, found by `link` from the device's descriptor; `knobOf` and `segmentValue` on the page). A linear segment is an even sweep on the knob and is drawn straight. Existing linear lanes on such parameters change sound: before, they went straight in the unit (a cutoff ramp spent most of its time in the high frequencies). Volume lanes keep the fader's travel (#389), pan lanes their unit.
